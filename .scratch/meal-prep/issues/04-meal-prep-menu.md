# What is on the launch meal-prep menu?

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 02

## Question

What Meals, Options, Add-ons and Packs do we launch with, at what starting prices, and what information does each Meal carry (description, portion size, allergens, ingredients, photo, heating and storage)? Starting point: the catering menu dishes and 1207 Kitchen's prices (singles £6–£15). Decide the Pack sizes and their discounts, and which allergen and ingredient data the kitchen must supply before launch.

## Answer

Decided with the user on 2026-11-26. Every value below is seed data for the Admin Dashboard and can be changed there.

**Launch Meals** (one 500 g Portion each):

| Meal | Option (protein) | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Jollof Rice | none / chicken / beef / turkey | £6 / £10 / £12 / £15 |
| Fried Rice | none / chicken / beef / turkey | £6 / £10 / £12 / £15 |
| Egusi Soup | none, assorted meat and fish included | £13 |
| Efo Riro | none, assorted meat and fish included | £15 |
| Okra Stew | none, assorted meat and fish included | £13 |
| Bitter Leaf Soup | none, assorted meat and fish included | £13 |
| Ayamase | none, assorted meat included | £13 |
| Assorted Meat Stew | none, assorted meat included | £12 |

Prices follow 1207 Kitchen where a dish matches. Okra and Bitter Leaf are priced with the soups. Yam Porridge and Abula were dropped in favour of Okra Stew and Bitter Leaf Soup. Ogbono and Peppersoup may come later if customers ask.

**Add-ons** (never discounted by a Pack), offered per Meal from a list set in the dashboard:
- Swallows (soups and stews): Eba £3, Amala £3, Semo £3, Pounded Yam £3.50
- Plantain £3 (all Meals); Moi Moi £2.50 (rice dishes); extra protein £4 (all Meals); Puff Puff, 6 pieces, £3 (all Meals)

**Packs:** 5 Meals at 10% off, 10 Meals at 15% off (both set in the dashboard). Any mix, repeats allowed. The discount applies to each Meal's price including its chosen protein (5 × Jollof with chicken = £45). Add-ons can be in the same Order at full price but don't count toward the Pack.

**Orders:** must contain at least one Meal. A minimum spend is decided with the delivery rules.

**Each Meal carries:** name, description, photo(s), Portion weight, price, ingredients with allergens in bold, the 14-allergen tick-list, a "may contain" line only where there's a real cross-contamination risk, spice level, halal (only if the meat supplier is certified), storage and reheating instructions, Shelf life in days, and available / sold out. No nutrition information at launch.

**Allergen check:** the kitchen lead enters ingredients and allergens for each Meal, checking every packet label (stock cubes and spice blends especially). A Meal can't be marked available until "allergens checked by … on …" is filled in.

**Photos:** before launch, one single-portion photo in the real container on a plain background, plus one wider shot, per Meal. A missing photo shows a branded placeholder and doesn't block a sale.

**Shelf life:** starts at 2 days use-by from the Batch's cook date for every Meal. Confirm or lengthen it with Manchester City Council during registration.
