# OlaJesu — Domain Glossary

**Meal**
A single portioned, freshly cooked ready meal from the meal-prep menu, delivered locally (never frozen or shipped by courier). Can be ordered on its own or as part of a Pack.

**Option**
The one choice a Meal may offer where it matters (e.g. the protein in Jollof). A Meal has at most one Option.

**Add-on**
An extra item added alongside a Meal (e.g. plantain, extra protein), priced separately.

**Batch**
One cooking run of a Meal. Its cook date sets the use-by date printed on each pack's label.

**Pack**
A fixed-size bundle of Meals (e.g. 5 or 10) where the Customer chooses each Meal. Costs less per Meal than buying the same Meals singly; the discount is set in the Admin Dashboard.

**Order**
A one-time purchase of Meals and/or Packs, paid by card at checkout. Distinct from a Catering Enquiry.

**Catering Enquiry**
A request for a quote for an event, sent via the catering form. Not paid online; no price is shown up front.

**Subscription** _(later phase)_
A recurring Order placed automatically on a schedule. Not part of the one-time ordering phase.

**Customer**
A person who places an Order. A Customer may order as a guest or with an Account.

**Account**
An optional login a Customer is encouraged (never required) to create, to make repeat ordering smoother. Required only for Subscriptions.

**Admin Dashboard**
Where OlaJesu staff manage Orders, Meals, Packs and prices. Prices and the menu are changed here, not in code.
